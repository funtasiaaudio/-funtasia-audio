Design Principles

Designed with intention.

01

Learn from analog.
Design for digital.

Useful ideas can come from anywhere. Reproducing hardware itself is not the goal.

02

Only what the sound needs.

Processing is included because it has a musical or functional reason to be there.

03

Meaningful choices stay yours.

Unnecessary decisions are reduced. Meaningful tradeoffs remain in the user's hands.

04

Experience in the defaults.
Freedom in the controls.

Defaults begin from real production experience while the controllable range remains yours.

Our Story

Another choice.

Many companies and developers have created remarkable tools inspired by analog hardware. We respect that work, and we are not trying to replace it.

FUNtasia Audio began with a different question: what happens when an audio tool is designed from the beginning for a digital environment?

We learn from useful acoustic behavior wherever it comes from, while allowing digital tools to follow their own logic. That can mean solving digital filter behavior directly, making meaningful processing choices visible, or building new ways to shape how sound is generated and transformed.

We are not trying to make the only answer. We are building another choice.
